💬 30-Minute Architecture Walkthrough Interview Guide (Click to Expand) ▾

Q1: Why did you not let the LLM generate SQL directly?
Answer: "Raw LLM-generated SQL creates severe SQL injection vulnerabilities, syntax unpredictability across database engines, and hallucinated calculations. Instead, our system uses the LLM solely as a semantic parser to output a strongly typed Pydantic QueryIntent AST. Parameterized queries are then safely compiled by Python, ensuring 100% data fidelity and zero hallucination."

Q2: How does the system handle high load and scaling to millions of tickets?
Answer: "Our architecture separates read analytics from transactional ingestion. For millions of rows: (1) Replace SQLite with PostgreSQL or ClickHouse with columnar indexing; (2) Introduce Redis result caching for frequent queries; (3) Offload heavy statistical anomaly batch scans to Celery/Redis workers."

Q3: How do you detect anomalies without expensive machine learning?
Answer: "We implement 4 statistical and business rule engines: (1) Tukey's IQR ($Q_3 + 1.5 \times \text{IQR}$) for resolution time outliers, robust against skewed distributions; (2) SLA breach engine for High/Critical tickets unresolved past 24 hours; (3) 95th-percentile thresholding for response latency; (4) CSAT floor alerting for ratings $\le 2$."
